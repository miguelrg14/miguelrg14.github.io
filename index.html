    About me

    I studied Computer Science and Video Game Engineering at ESNE in Oviedo and graduated in Video Game Design and Development at UDIT in Madrid in 2025.

    I program mostly in C# with Unity, and I've also worked in C++ with OpenGL and Unreal Engine. In team projects I've been a producer and I've usually taken care of the UI and the sound, so I'm used to sitting between design, art and code.

    Away from the engine I draw, make 3D models, produce music and DJ. I like learning new things and I try to do each one a little better than the last.

    I speak Spanish and Galician natively, and English.
